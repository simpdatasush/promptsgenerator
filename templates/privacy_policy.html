Privacy Policy

How SuperPrompter AI collects, processes, and protects your information.
Updated: October 2026

Client-Side Processing Notice: Interactive document editing and visual crops (such as Photo & Signature extraction and PDF editing) run locally in your web browser. Uploaded source files are processed in-session and are not permanently archived on our database servers.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. When you use SuperPrompter AI, we collect only the information strictly necessary to provide our productivity and generative tools:

  • Account & Authentication Data: If you register or authenticate, we store your email address, username, and encrypted credential hashes.
  • User Prompts & Operational Inputs: Text prompts submitted to generation engines (e.g., prompters, scenario analyzers) to return immediate model responses.
  • Transient Document Data: Images or PDFs processed through our extractor or editor modules. These assets are converted directly on client memory or sent transiently to vision inference endpoints without permanent persistence.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Standard web analytics including non-identifying browser type, session response latency, and error trace IDs to monitor system uptime.

2. How We Use AI Models

Our tools utilize state-of-the-art multimodal artificial intelligence models via official APIs (such as Google GenAI / Gemini APIs). By using our application, you acknowledge that:

  • User prompts and document snippets are routed securely via encrypted TLS transmission to upstream model providers strictly for inference execution.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your prompts or uploaded document data to third-party data brokers.
  • API integrations operate under standard enterprise terms that do not use submitted API payloads to train foundational public models.

3. Data Retention & Security

We implement technical and organizational controls to protect your data:

  • No Cloud Document Storage: Extracted candidate images, signatures, and modified PDFs are converted and downloaded client-side. We do not maintain a permanent document repository of personal forms.
  • Session Cleansing: Any temporary cache or server sessions can be cleared instantly using the built-in Refresh or Reset tools.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your browser, our application, and downstream inference endpoints use HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.

4. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and Indian DPDP standards), you retain full rights regarding your data:

  • You may request the deletion of your account and associated session credentials at any time.
  • You may opt out of storing local browser storage (session state, canvas caches) by clearing your browser cache.
  • You may discontinue using any AI-assisted auto-detection feature and rely exclusively on local manual editing tools.

5. Contact Us

For inquiries, feedback, or data privacy requests concerning this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our official developer channels on SuperPrompter.
